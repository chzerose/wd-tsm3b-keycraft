FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is KeyCraft?

A school-project website where you can browse keycaps, design your own, and preview them on a live keyboard.

Is the checkout real?

No. The checkout is a prototype, so no payment is taken and no order is placed.

How do I make my own keycap?

Open Customize, pick the text, colors, font, material, image and sound, choose which key it goes on, then add it to the cart.

What if two keycaps use the same key?

Both stay in your cart list, but the keyboard shows the first one you added. If you remove it, the next one takes its place.

Where are my account and cart saved?

Only in your own browser on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes them, and they will not appear on other devices.

Are my uploaded images sent anywhere?

No. Uploaded images stay in your browser.

How do I switch between dark and light mode?

Use the moon/sun button in the top bar. The site remembers your choice.

Why is there no sound?

Check that the Sound button in the top bar says ON and that your device is not muted.

TERMS OF USE

  2. No real transactions. Prices are examples. No payments are processed and no products are shipped.
  3. Accounts and data. Account details, carts and settings are stored locally in your browser. Do not use a password that you use elsewhere.
  4. Your uploads. Only upload images you have the right to use. They stay on your device.
  5. Fair use. Please do not misuse the site or try to break it.
  6. Changes. The site and these terms may change at any time as the project develops.
